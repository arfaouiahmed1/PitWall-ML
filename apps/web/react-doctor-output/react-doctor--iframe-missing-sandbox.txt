Rule: react-doctor/iframe-missing-sandbox
Severity: warning
Category: Security
Count: 1

Combining `allow-scripts` & `allow-same-origin` lets the iframe remove its own sandbox, defeating the protection.

Suggestion: Add `sandbox=""` or a curated value so embedded pages cannot get full access to your site by default.

Curl with no cache & follow the canonical fix and false positive check recipe before fixing: https://react.doctor/docs/rules/react-doctor/iframe-missing-sandbox
